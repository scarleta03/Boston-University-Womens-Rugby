Who we are

Our story

From first practice to first cap

BU Women's Rugby is a member of Rugby Northeast under USA Rugby, competing in the Division I Northeast Women's Collegiate Rugby Conference. Most of our roster had never touched a rugby ball before their first semester here — every new player is paired one-on-one with a veteran who teaches them the game from the ground up, position by position, tackle by tackle.

We accept new members all year round, no experience required. As head coach Jess Glauser puts it, on this field "you're encouraged to pick up space, and you're encouraged to be loud." That culture is why we've been called the most inclusive club sport on campus.

In fall 2024, after Team USA's bronze medal at the Paris Olympics, nearly 40 new members joined the club in a single season — about triple a typical year. The growth changed the size of our squad, not the way we treat a first practice.

What we stand for

Three things every rookie hears in week one

01

No experience, no problem

Most of the roster started here. New players are paired with a veteran mentor and taught the game from first principles — rules, positions, and contact, in that order.

02

Take up space, be loud

The culture Coach Glauser has built is explicit: this is a field where you're encouraged to be seen and heard, on the pitch and off it.

03

Earn your minutes

There are no cuts. Playing time is decided by who shows up to practice, not by a single tryout day in September.
