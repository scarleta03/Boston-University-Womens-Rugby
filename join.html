How it works

Three steps, and step one is just showing up

No cuts, no experience required, no gear to buy before your first day.

01

Come to tryouts

Nickerson Field, September 16, 2026, 6:30–9:00 PM. Wear cleats and clothes you can move in — we'll handle the rest.

02

Sign the roster

Fill out our short interest form and USA Rugby membership so you're covered to play in matches once you're ready.

03

Make the squad

Everyone who trains consistently gets match minutes. Playing time is earned at practice, not decided by a single tryout day.

Practices run every Monday and Wednesday evening, rain or shine, at Nickerson Field.

What to bring

Day-one checklist

Nothing on this list needs to be expensive or rugby-specific.

  • Cleats soccer or rugby boots, no metal toe studs
  • Clothes you can move in shorts or leggings, athletic top
  • Mouthguard required for contact — a basic boil-and-bite works
  • Water bottle practice runs rain or shine
  • Layer for weather Nickerson Field is exposed to wind off the river
  • An open mind rules and positions are taught from day one
Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need any rugby experience?

No. Most of the current roster had never played before their first semester. Every new player is paired with a veteran mentor who teaches the rules, positions, and contact technique from scratch.

What if I can't make the September 16 tryout?

We accept new members all year round — tryout day is just the easiest place to start. Email buwrfc@bu.edu or message the club on Instagram to find a practice to drop into.

Will I actually get playing time?

There are no cuts, and minutes are earned at practice rather than decided on tryout day. Fall runs 15-a-side league matches; spring shifts to faster 7s tournaments, which spread minutes across more of the roster.

What's the time commitment?

Practice is Wednesdays 6:30–9:00 PM and Monday evenings 9:00–11:00 PM at Nickerson Field, with occasional indoor sessions at the BU Track & Tennis Center once it gets cold, plus weekend matches during the season. Attendance expectations pick up as match day gets closer.

Do I need to join USA Rugby or pay dues?

Yes — a USA Rugby membership is required to play in matches for insurance and eligibility purposes, and covers you across the season. Details are on the sign-up form linked above.

Still have questions? Reach out before tryout day and we'll walk you through it.
