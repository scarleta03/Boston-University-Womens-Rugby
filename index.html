Who we are

Boston's most inclusive club sport

BU Women's Rugby is a member of Rugby Northeast under USA Rugby, competing in the Division I Northeast Women's Collegiate Rugby Conference. Most of our roster had never touched a rugby ball before their first semester here — every new player is paired one-on-one with a veteran who teaches them the game from the ground up.

We accept new members all year round, no experience required. As head coach Jess Glauser puts it, on this field "you're encouraged to pick up space, and you're encouraged to be loud."

What we do

Training, matches, and the stuff in between

Whether you're chasing a starting spot or just want to hit something twice a week, there's a place on this roster for you.

Mon & Wed

Practice

Skills, contact technique, and conditioning at Nickerson Field. Every rookie is paired one-on-one with a veteran for hands-on mentorship from day one.

Fall: 15s · Spring: 7s

Match day

Fall means 15-a-side league play in the D1 Northeast Women's Collegiate Rugby Conference. Spring shifts to fast, open 7s tournaments across the region.

Year-round

Team life

A team that recruits year-round, welcomes every skill level, and has built a reputation as one of the most inclusive club sports at BU.

Featured in BU Today: after Team USA's bronze medal at the 2024 Paris Olympics, nearly 40 new members joined the club in a single fall — about triple a typical year.

Get involved

Joining is three steps, and step one is just showing up

No cuts, no experience required, no gear to buy before your first day.

01

Come to tryouts

Nickerson Field, September 16, 2026, 6:30–9:00 PM. Wear cleats and clothes you can move in — we'll handle the rest.

02

Sign the roster

Fill out our short interest form and USA Rugby membership so you're covered to play in matches once you're ready.

03

Make the squad

Everyone who trains consistently gets match minutes. Playing time is earned at practice, not decided by a single tryout day.

Practices run every Monday and Wednesday evening, rain or shine, at Nickerson Field.
