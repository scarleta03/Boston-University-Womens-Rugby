Announcement

Fall 2026 tryouts are set for September 16

Aug 20, 2026

Tryouts for the 2026 fall season run from 6:30 to 9:00 PM at Nickerson Field. Come in cleats and clothes you can move in — the coaching staff will cover rules, positions, and basic contact technique for anyone who's never played before.

There are no cuts. Everyone who shows up leaves with a spot to keep training, and playing time from there is earned at Monday and Wednesday practice.

Feature

Meet head coach Jess Glauser

Aug 12, 2026

Most of the roster Jess Glauser coaches had never played rugby before college. That's by design: new players are paired one-on-one with a veteran teammate from their first practice, learning the game position by position rather than being thrown onto the pitch cold.

"You're encouraged to pick up space, and you're encouraged to be loud," Glauser says of the culture on the field — a line that's become something close to a team motto, on and off the pitch.

Community

Nearly 40 new members joined the club this fall

Oct 2, 2025

BU Today featured the club's growth after Team USA's bronze medal finish at the 2024 Paris Olympics put rugby in front of a much wider audience. Interest showed up immediately: about 40 new members joined in a single fall, roughly triple a typical year.

The club's answer to the growth wasn't to raise the bar for who could join — it was to lean harder into the one-on-one mentorship system that's always defined the program.
