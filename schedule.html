Practice

Weekly, September – December

Practice runs Wednesdays, 6:30–9:00 PM and Monday evenings, 9:00–11:00 PM, at Nickerson Field, rain or shine. As the weather turns colder, some sessions move indoors to the BU Track & Tennis Center — the subscribed calendar always has the current location.

Fall tryouts are Wednesday, September 16, 6:30–9:00 PM, in that same practice slot — just show up.

Spring — 7s tournaments

Regional 7s circuit

The spring 7s schedule is released in January once tournament fields are set. It'll appear here and on the subscribed calendar as soon as dates are confirmed.

Want to be on the pitch for these? Fall tryouts are open to every skill level.
